How NexAlc Calculators Work: Formulas & Sources

Every calculator on NexAlc uses standard, auditable equations—no black-box estimates, no proprietary guesswork, and no paywalls.

100% Local Browser Calculations — No Sign-Up, No Data Stored

Every number you enter into NexAlc—salaries, home prices, body measurements, birth dates, or bill amounts—is calculated locally inside your own web browser using client-side JavaScript. No account is required, and none of your calculator inputs are transmitted to, logged by, or stored on NexAlc servers.

1. Mortgage Amortization & Regional Compounding

The Mortgage Calculator computes fixed-rate monthly principal and interest (P&I) payments using the standard present-value annuity formula:

M = P × [ r(1 + r)^n ] / [ (1 + r)^n − 1 ]
  • P = Principal loan amount (Home Price − Down Payment).
  • n = Total number of monthly payments (Loan Term in Years × 12).
  • US, UK, and Australia compounding: Nominal annual rate R is divided monthly: r = R / 12.
  • Canada semi-annual compounding: Under Canada's Interest Act, fixed-rate mortgages compound semi-annually, so the effective monthly rate is computed as r = (1 + R / 2)^(1 / 6) − 1.
  • Total PITI & PMI: Optional annual property tax, homeowners insurance, HOA dues, and Private Mortgage Insurance (applied automatically when loan-to-value exceeds 80%) are divided by 12 and added to P&I for a complete monthly payment breakdown.

References: CFPB Loan Estimate Amortization Standards; Canada Interest Act (R.S.C., 1985, c. I-15). See also Glossary: Amortization & PMI.

2. Body Mass Index (BMI) & Clinical Thresholds

The BMI Calculator implements the World Health Organization (WHO) and U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) adult formulas for metric and imperial units:

Metric: BMI = weight (kg) / [ height (m) ]² Imperial: BMI = [ weight (lb) / ( height (in) )² ] × 703
  • Standard adult WHO / CDC bands: Underweight (< 18.5), Healthy Weight (18.5 – 24.9), Overweight (25.0 – 29.9), and Obese (≥ 30.0).
  • Healthy weight range: Derived by inverting the BMI formula for the user's height across 18.5 and 24.9 kg/m².
  • Body fat reference: Where body fat estimates are shown alongside BMI, calculations follow the published Deurenberg equation incorporating BMI, age, and biological sex as an informational estimate rather than a clinical scan.

References: World Health Organization (WHO) Adult BMI Classification; CDC Healthy Weight Guidelines. See also Glossary: BMI.

3. Percentage, Change & Discount Math

The Percentage Calculator handles proportion, percentage change, percentage difference, and retail discount calculations directly in IEEE-754 double-precision floating point with clean rounding for display:

Percent of Value: (P / 100) × X Percentage Change: ((New − Old) / |Old|) × 100 Percentage Diff: (|A − B| / ((A + B) / 2)) × 100 Sale Price (d% off): Original × (1 − d / 100)

Need quick examples? Read How to Calculate Percentage Increase and Decrease or What is a Good Discount Percentage?

4. Currency Exchange Rate Sourcing

The Currency Converter pulls live interbank mid-market reference exchange rates from established public FX rate feeds (anchored to European Central Bank and global market reference rates). Conversions between any two currencies are computed by multiplying the source amount by the current mid-market cross rate.

  • Mid-market benchmark: Displayed rates reflect the midpoint between wholesale buy and sell quotes without added commission.
  • Bank & card spreads: Retail banks, card networks, and currency exchange booths typically add a 1% to 10% spread above mid-market rates, so live transaction totals may differ slightly from interbank quotes.

Reference: European Central Bank (ECB) Reference Rates & open interbank FX market feeds. See Glossary: Mid-Market Exchange Rate.

5. Tip & Bill Split Arithmetic

The Tip Calculator computes gratuity directly from the bill amount and chosen percentage, then splits the combined total evenly across any party size:

Tip Amount = Bill × (Tip % / 100) Total Bill = Bill + Tip Amount Per Person = Total Bill / Number of People

See also Tipping Guide by Service Type.

6. Calendar Age & Countdown Timing

The Age Calculator and Countdown Timer rely on exact Gregorian calendar date math and your device's system clock:

  • Exact years, months, and days: Calculated calendar-month by calendar-month using actual month lengths (28, 29, 30, or 31 days) rather than a rough 30.44-day average.
  • Leap-year precision: Properly accounts for leap days on years divisible by 4, excluding century years not divisible by 400 (e.g., 2000 was a leap year; 1900 was not).
  • Live countdown delta: Computes the exact millisecond difference between the target timestamp and Date.now() on your local device.
