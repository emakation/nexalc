Tipping Etiquette by Country: Global Gratuity Guide

Know when to tip, how much to give, and when tipping is considered impolite across destinations worldwide.

Quick Rule: In the US & Canada, 18–20% is expected in restaurants. In most of Europe, service is included and rounding up is customary. In Japan & South Korea, tipping is generally unexpected and can cause confusion.
